# Master profile

Copy this file to me/profile.md and fill it in. Every claim needs `(verified: yes)` or `(verified: no)`.
Only `verified: yes` claims may appear in a generated resume or cover letter.

## Contact
Name, city, and contact details go in me/profile.md only. Never put them in a tracked file.

## Summary
One or two sentences, in the person's own words. (verified: yes/no)
